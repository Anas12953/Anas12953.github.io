Al Ebtehal Events Calendar

A school year, in view.

A bilingual school-year calendar for Al Ebtehal KG, organizing events from August 2026 to July 2027.

My role
Concept, visual direction, review and testing, with AI assistance
Tools & technology
Microsoft Excel · Google Sheets · Calendar design
Context
Spreadsheet tools / 2026–2027
01

The need

The kindergarten needed a readable view of school-wide events and reminders across the academic year. The brief covered August 2026 through July 2027.

02

The approach

The workbook places months into a consistent calendar layout with space for daily entries. Visual hierarchy, readable dates and clear boundaries make the year easier to scan.

03

My contribution

I directed the concept and appearance, then reviewed and refined the result with AI assistance. This is a spreadsheet project, presented here through its own portfolio page.

04

What I learned

Good interface thinking also applies to a workbook: group related information, keep the structure consistent, and leave enough room for the content people need to enter.
